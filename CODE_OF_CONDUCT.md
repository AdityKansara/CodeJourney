# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, we pledge to make participation in this project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Insults, sarcasm, or condescending language
- Posting harmful or hateful content
- Disrespecting others in discussion or contribution feedback

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying expectations and promoting a safe environment. Maintainers will respond to issues and pull requests respectfully.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and repository discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the repository owner.
